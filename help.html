How to play the Collector

Real MyVMK items drop into the collect channel around the clock. Be quick, build a collection, customize your card, and cause a little character chaos along the way.

1The core loop: grab the drop

📦 Items drop in the collect channel every few minutes — rarer ones get a flashy announcement.
⚡ Type collect in chat. The first 3 players within 5 seconds each keep a copy (the 2nd and 3rd get roasted).
🏴‍☠️ Or type blackbeard during that 5s window to steal the drop from a random collector. (10 min cooldown — and their doodle may fight back with /petshop.)

2Rarities

Common Uncommon Rare Epic Legendary

Every item in the 13,000-item MyVMK catalog has a curated rarity. Collect enough of one tier and you earn titles for your card — 10 Legendaries makes you a Beginner Legend. Check /item to look anything up, and /leaderboard to see who's ahead.

3Two currencies

🪙 Club Coins — a 1-in-10 chance every time you collect, plus /daily check-ins. Spend them in the shops below.
❄️ Yeti Credits — also drop while collecting. Spend one on /yeti to make it rain 3 items for the whole channel (you pick yours first!). Coins can cover most purchases when credits run dry — Club Coins spend first, Yeti tops up.

📅 /daily pays 5 🪙 and grows with your streak: +1/day every 5 days (days 1–5 pay 5, days 6–10 pay 6…). Resets at midnight Eastern — miss a day and the streak starts over.

4Shops, cooking & crafting

/shop — the Coin Shop: crates from 1 🪙 (common) to 15 🪙 (legendary), each a guaranteed item of that rarity.
/ned — Ned's Daily Shop: one item per rarity, restocked daily, priced 2/4/8/12/20.
/remy — Anyone Can Cook: throw any 3 items in the pot for a random one back — usually uncommon, sometimes far better. Rarer ingredients improve the odds. No cooldown!
/combine — fuse duplicate Magic pins into one starred Magic (up to ★5).
/crates — open Legendary Crates earned by supporting the bot (see the bottom of this page).

5Your collector card

Your profile is a rendered card: stats, rarity counts, earned titles, and a showcase of up to 8 featured items (15 with the Gallery layout). Featured items can't be stolen or lost!

/profile — view your card (or anyone's). /inventory & /recent list your items.
/feature / /unfeature — pick your showcase.
/theme — 30+ card themes, from free to animated. /setbio and /setcolor finish the look.

💻 Prefer clicking to typing? The web portal does all of this with a live preview — sign in with Discord, browse your full inventory, and design your card there.

6Character chaos (each with its own cooldown — check /cooldown)

Luck & loot:

4h/walle — a mystery find only you can open: trash, common, or rare.
2h/hades — a 5-second deal: rare+ item… or your next collect is claimed.
4h/maleficent — battle the dragon: 30% to win, and 70% of wins are rare+.
6h/gator — the Gator Waiter serves a random player an item.
3h/russell — Wilderness Explorer quest: 6 items in an hour → 3 🪙 + 3 ❄️.
6h/ursula — a 5-spawn deal: boosted odds & freeze-immunity, but missed drops cost items. Finish it for a wish.
3h/presto — trades one random item of yours for another of the same rarity.

Mischief & duels:

1h/elsa — freeze a player for 15 min so they can't collect (anonymous!). /frozen checks the thaw.
4h/yzma — "WRONG LEVERRR!" Their next collected item turns to ash (anonymous).
4h/stitch — sabotage the next drop: timing, rarity, or it relocates somewhere in the server.
6h/scar — 50/50 duel: winner takes half the loser's Yeti Credits.
1h/rps — Rock Paper Scissors for one of the loser's items.
10m/petshop — just plundered? Call your doodle to maul the pirate and take it back.

Bending the spawns:

1h/flort — delay the next drop ~2.5 min to make it guaranteed epic+ (you can't grab that one).
30m/esmeralda — surrender an item to see the next 5 drops.
4h/dash — server-wide super-speed: drops come twice as fast for 30 min.
4h/genie — a 75-second spawn frenzy: back-to-back drops as fast as you can grab.
2h/edna — dahling, the next 4 drops are all the same item.
90m/vanellope — glitch! Skip the next drop entirely.
90m/slinkydog — spring back! The next drop repeats the previous item.
8h/cinderella — pay 1 🪙 to reserve a guaranteed grab on the next drop, pirate-proof and freeze-proof.
3h/baymax — heal yourself: clears a freeze, a curse, or a Hades debt.

⚡ Skip the wait: catch a command on cooldown and the reply offers a coin-priced skip button — it buys one extra use while the original cooldown keeps ticking.

7Trading & giving

/trade — offer a swap (accept/decline), or hit 🔄 on anyone's inventory or profile.
/market — browse what everyone owns to find trade partners.
/gift — hand an item straight to a friend, no race.
/giveaway — toss an item to the channel: first to type collect keeps it (no pirates allowed).

8Support the club ☕

The bot runs 24/7 on a real server. Optional supporter perks say thank-you for helping keep it alive — Legendary Crates, coins, an exclusive animated theme, and custom card backgrounds. Run /supporter in Discord for the tiers and your personal donation code, and open what you've earned with /crates. Collecting is and stays 100% free.
